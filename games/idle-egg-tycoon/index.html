Idle Egg Tycoon

A free mobile monster creation game: merge chickens, hatch eggs and grow your own factory.

The download link will appear here when the game is released.

Create monsters, starting with one egg

In Idle Egg Tycoon, every monster starts with a chicken and an egg. Buy chickens to keep production moving, then tap eggs to reveal the creatures inside. Each new discovery adds to your growing monster collection.

This free mobile game combines factory building with the surprise of creating monsters. Choose what your machines do, sell the monsters you make for coins and reinvest to expand your factory. Produce, discover and upgrade as you go.

How to grow your monster factory

Merge chickens for better eggs

Combine two chickens of the same level. A stronger chicken produces more valuable eggs and helps your factory grow.

Hatch monsters with different powers

Tap eggs to discover their monsters, then try the Poison, Fire, Volt and Freeze powers offered by your machines.

Grow your monster collection

Use the collection book to revisit the eggs and creatures you've found. Sell monsters for coins to keep improving your factory.

About Idle Egg Tycoon

Will Idle Egg Tycoon be free?

How do you create monsters?

Produce eggs with your chickens, merge chickens of the same level, then tap the eggs to reveal monsters. Your machines let you try different powers.
